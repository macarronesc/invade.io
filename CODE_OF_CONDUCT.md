# Code of conduct

invade.io is for everyone who wants to play it, improve it or learn from it. In
issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers; answer questions patiently and explain your review comments.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or sharing of private information.
- Keep reports of bugs and vulnerabilities factual; vulnerabilities go through
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never a public issue.

Maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions and block participants who
break these rules. To report a problem, email
[alegandro2507@gmail.com](mailto:alegandro2507@gmail.com); reports are handled privately.
